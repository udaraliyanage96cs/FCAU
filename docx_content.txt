Key Stakeholders and Their Roles in IIMS
Food Control Administration Unit: Develops and maintains the IIMS platform; sets national standards for digital inspection.
Authorized Officers of Food Safety (MOH, PHI, F & DI): Primary system users responsible for conducting inspections and entering data.
Food Laboratories: Receive digital sample requests, upload test results, and integrate findings into IIMS.

Inspection Workflow — System Overview
The IIMS inspection workflow guides authorized officers through a structured digital process — from logging in, selecting sampling activities, entering data, submitting samples, through 
to viewing
 lab results and downloading reports.
IIMS System — How to add a sample to the system?
The system displays a list of all submitted samples, allowing officers to track status and manage records efficiently.
Once lab results are available in IIMS, the following actions can be performed:
Edit — Modify sample details if required.
View Lab Result — Review the full laboratory report online.
Request Hard Copy from Lab — Submit a formal request for a printed copy of the report.
View — Open the sample record for detailed viewing.
Download Report — Save or print the report for records.
Premises Registration
To register a food premise, click the Food Premises option in the IIMS navigation menu. This opens the premises registration module where new premises can be added to the national registry.
Upon successful entry and submission of premises registration details, the IIMS system automatically generates a unique serial number for the registered premises. This number serves as the official identifier for all future inspections and records related to 
that
 premises.
